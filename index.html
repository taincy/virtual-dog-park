Dog Encyclopedia

Each card shows one breed with its size, personality, and everyday care.

Beagle

Size
Small to medium, about 10–11 kg and roughly 33–41 cm tall.
Personality
Curious, cheerful, and led by its nose. It loves company and follows scents.
Everyday care
A daily walk plus time to sniff, a secure garden, and a weekly brush.

Golden Retriever

Size
Large, about 25–34 kg and roughly 51–61 cm tall.
Personality
Gentle, patient, and eager to please, which makes it a popular family dog.
Everyday care
Long walks, fetch games, and brushing two or three times a week.

Poodle

Size
Comes in several sizes; the miniature stands about 28–38 cm tall.
Personality
Bright, alert, and quick to learn. It enjoys training games and attention.
Everyday care
Regular brushing, a haircut every few weeks, and short but frequent walks.
